## The context

A government website serves both the public looking for information and the people responsible for maintaining it. During my July–October 2025 internship at Dinas Komunikasi dan Informatika Kota Metro, I contributed to web systems supporting those two sides.

This work took place inside an existing organization and its municipal platforms, as part of my role as a Fullstack Web Developer Intern.

## My role and contribution

My contributions included:

- Public-facing pages for municipal web systems.
- Administrative interfaces used to manage information.
- CRUD features supporting data and content updates.
- Content-management workflows connecting the administrative and public sides.

The public portal is linked as the organization’s website. My role was to contribute specific components within the existing systems.

## Working across the stack

The internship stack included **Laravel and PHP**, **React and TypeScript**, **Tailwind CSS**, and **MySQL**. This work gave me a practical setting for moving between interfaces, application behavior, and persistent data.

The public page is one part of that work. The administrative flows that create and maintain its content also need attention, because they affect how the website is operated after a change is delivered.

## Working within an existing system

Contributing to an established platform means understanding its structure before changing it. My work had to fit the organization’s existing pages, administrative interfaces, and content workflows.

That experience broadened how I think about web development: a useful interface also depends on the routines and data-management work behind it.

## Related contributions

The internship also included [Bapokting]({{< relref "projects/bapokting.md" >}}), where my work focused on market-data administration and price-chart visualization. Metro Kita and limited MetroMaps-related work were also part of the internship experience.

The portfolio keeps the named project pages focused on the contributions I can describe specifically.

## Public access and scope

The project link opens the public Metro City government portal. Internal administrative interfaces are not reproduced in this portfolio.

This case study presents my internship contribution and the technology used in that work. It does not represent ownership of the entire municipal platform.
